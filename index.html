Account & Data Deletion Request

Google Play Policy Compliant Account Erasure Portal

1 Overview

At SoloTrades, we respect your right to privacy and total ownership over your data. In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users can request the permanent deletion of their account, business profile, and associated cloud/local records at any time.

2 How to Delete Your Account and Data

You can delete your data using either of the following methods:

Method A: Immediate In-App Deletion

  1. Open the SoloTrades app on your device.
  2. Navigate to Settings.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Sign Out (this permanently clears locally cached session credentials).
  4. You can also delete any individual customer, invoice, job, or estimate by tapping the respective delete button with confirmation.
  5. To erase all local application database storage instantly, go to your phone's Settings → Apps → SoloTrades → Storage → Clear Data.

Method B: Submit a Permanent Deletion Request by Email

If you created an account with your email or Google Sign-In and wish to completely delete your cloud profile and records from our Firebase servers:

  1. Send an email from your registered email address to: growwalletofficial@gmail.com.
  2. Use the Subject line: [Data Deletion Request] - SoloTrades.
  3. Include your registered email address in the message body.
Once received, your account credentials and any synced cloud data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

3 What Data is Deleted?

Upon completing your deletion request:

  • Account Identifiers: Your Firebase Authentication user account, email address, and unique user ID are permanently deleted.
  • Cloud Synced Records: All business profiles, invoices, estimates, client database records, and job entries stored in Cloud Firestore are permanently erased.
  • Storage Files: Any attached job photos, logos, or generated PDF documents in cloud storage are purged.
Retention Period: No personal or operational data is retained after deletion. Once deleted, your account cannot be recovered.
